GENERATED CONTENT
-----------------
- All textures (asphalt, facades, ground, water, sky, signs) and all city buildings, trees, lamps and road furniture are generated procedurally
  in the browser by the game code. No third-party photographs or texture downloads are used.
- Sound effects and music are synthesised with the Web Audio API; no audio files are bundled.

CITIES
------
Cities are artistic environments inspired by the character of each city. They are not geographic reproductions and do not depict
exact landmarks or street layouts.
