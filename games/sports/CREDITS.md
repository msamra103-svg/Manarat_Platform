# Sports V19.3

Athletes, articulated rigs, venue geometry and procedural materials are original project code. Renderer: locally bundled Three.js (MIT; existing `games/vendor/three.min.js`). No externally loaded model, library or font. Football seating backdrop reuses the AI-generated stadium from V19.1. Crowd audio references and original licenses remain in `games/football/CREDITS.md`.

Characters are real-time 3D game models, not footage or photoreal scans. The legacy pose atlas and `football.js` are retained for historical compatibility but not loaded by V19.3.
