# Football scene and audio

Stadium and player atlas: AI-generated for this project using the built-in image generation tool. Photographic simulation inspired by Lusail Stadium, not a photograph of a real match or identifiable professional players. The scene uses perspective animation and photographic sprites (2.5D), with a projected rotating football.

- crowd-cheer.mp3: “Crowd Cheer II” by FoolBoyMedia, CC0, https://freesound.org/people/FoolBoyMedia/sounds/397435/ — public HQ preview.
- crowd-miss.mp3: “crowd oh - disappointed” by mrrap4food, CC0, https://freesound.org/people/mrrap4food/sounds/619007/ — public HQ preview.
- Whistle and ball impact: generated at runtime with Web Audio; no third-party recording.

Prompts: stadium: realistic empty Qatar stadium inspired by Lusail, centered goal and detailed grass, evening crowd, no players; atlas: six photographic full-body poses of a burgundy-uniform striker (ready, wind-up, follow-through) and green goalkeeper (ready, left dive, right dive), transparent background.
