# Basketball — CHANGELOG

## V23.1 — 25 Oct 2026 (this package): reconstruction and integration

**Why:** the V23 package arrived half-merged. The new V23 modules and assets were present (`athletes.js`, `crowd.js`, `arena.js`, `audio.js`, `game.js`, rigged GLB athletes, crowd data, v23 tests, QA material), but `games/basketball/{scene.js, motion.js, index.html, basketball.css, DEVELOPMENT.md}` were still the V22 prototype files (their SHA-256 matched the V22 manifest). The page therefore still loaded the geometric mannequins from `games/sports/athlete.js`, none of the V23 modules were wired in, and `tests/basketball-motion-v23.cjs` failed (`COURT` undefined).

**What was done** (basketball-only; shared football/platform files remain byte-identical):
- **`motion.js` rebuilt** against the v23 test specification: FIBA court geometry; deterministic ball simulation (1/480 s steps that are exact ballistic polynomials; bisection time-of-impact against the rim torus, glass board, floor, padded walls and stanchion pad; restitution and friction with hollow-sphere spin coupling; net drag that slows and centres a swish; rolling); closed-form shot solver; `planMake` (always scores, ≥ 6 mm rim clearance); `planMiss` with four variants (front rim, back rim, side rim, glass-then-rim), filtered so they never score, contact the rim/board after the question, deviate from the make arc by ≤ 0.5 m/s (mostly along the arc), and come off the rim onto the floor; `shotBall` with the identical path up to the question and a C¹ blend (position and velocity continuous) onto the simulated miss; analytic dribble with exact floor and hand contacts; jump curve with ballistic flight, heel raise and landing absorption. 152/152 check groups pass.
- **`scene.js` rebuilt** (the `SportScene` interface `game.js`, the harness and the browser tests expect): quality tiers + runtime hooks (`setPixelRatio`, `thinCrowd`), PMREM room environment, key/fill/rim/spot lighting with soft shadows, loading through one `LoadingManager` with real progress, shader warm-up; choreography for seven athletes on one simulation clock — shooter (catch the pass, absorb, push into the dribble, dribble with the palm on the ball during contact, catch from the dribble, two-hand gather, knee dip, rise, set point, release slightly before the apex with the ball's velocity matching the launch velocity, gooseneck follow-through, landing, held follow-through during the question, reaction), close-out defender (low stance, late contest jump with the hand up but clear of the ball), two help defenders (stance, lateral slides with lead/trail foot steps and squeaks), rebounder (box-out readiness, plans a catch from the simulated ball path and runs/walks with speed-matched clips, two-hand catch, chest pass with follow-through; fetches a ball from the courtside cart when no catch is possible or the answer is pending), teammates (staggered, non-synchronised reactions). Procedural ball texture (eight panels, pebble bump), visible spin from the simulated angular velocity, 12-strand verlet net pushed by the ball, pending halo/fade, crowd and LED ribbon reactions, sports camera (establishing move, playable view, gentle push during flight, short celebration emphasis, smooth return; fixed under `prefers-reduced-motion`), `poseCheck()`/`snapshot()` for QA.
- **`index.html` and `basketball.css` rebuilt**: Arabic RTL interface with every element `game.js` drives; Tajawal fonts bundled; maroon/white HUD (assignment title, school, optional subject/teacher, basketball points kept separate from correct answers/pending/platform points); loading progress, slow-network notice, Arabic error with retry; welcome card; question card that keeps the frozen ball visible; outcome card placed beside the hoop, not over it; summary with the exact assignment title; 44 px+ touch targets; phone portrait and landscape layouts; visible focus; reduced-motion CSS.
- **Fixes found during visual/pose QA:** feet stay IK-planted until a lowered pelvis has recovered (prevented a 3 mm ankle dip when defenders stand up); guide hand lowered/opened in the follow-through; camera reframed so the dribble is not hidden by the shoot button.
- **Tests:** retired `tests/basketball-motion-v22.cjs` and `tests/basketball-scene-v22.cjs` (they tested the replaced prototype); all v23 suites, the shared football suites, 21/21 browser checks and the multi-round pose check pass (see `QA_RESULTS_BASKETBALL.md`).
- **QA evidence** in `qa/` replaced with screenshots, a video and measurements captured from this build.

## V23.0 — 25 Oct 2026 (previous session; files listed under "Rewritten" were missing from the delivered package and are reconstructed in V23.1)

Replaces the V22 motion prototype. Basketball-only; football and the shared `games/sports/*` files are byte-identical to V22.

### Characters and animation
- **Rigged, textured human athletes** from the Microsoft Rocketbox library (MIT) replace the geometric mannequins: 80-bone skeletons with full fingers and facial bones, photo-based faces, skin/cloth normal and roughness maps.
- Three distinct faces on the same basketball body (heads of Sports_Male_02/03 grafted onto Sports_Male_04 at the neck seam), maroon home kit and white away kit with front/back numbers.
- Seven athletes on court: shooter (#10), contesting defender (#5), two teammates (#7, #4), two defenders in stance (#8, #14), rebounder (#12).
- Layered animation: motion-capture clips (idle/breathing, look-around, celebration, clapping, restrained shrug/disappointment, walk, run) through `AnimationMixer` with cross-fades, plus a procedural layer: analytic two-bone IK for arms and legs, palm orientation and finger curl around the ball, spine bend/twist, head look-at, independent blinking.
- Jump shot driven by one shot clock: catch from the dribble → two-hand gather → knee dip → rise → take-off → set point → release slightly before the apex → wrist follow-through → landing absorption → recovery. Feet are IK-planted (no sliding); feet leave the floor only while airborne.
- Dribble: analytic hand–ball–floor cycle (push, floor contact, rebound, catch); the bounce sound fires at the exact floor-contact instant of the same clock (fixes the V22 dribble/sound mismatch).
- Defenders: low stance, lateral slides with sequential foot steps; the close-out defender rises and contests without touching the ball.
- Rebounder: plans a catch from the simulated ball path, moves with matched walk/run clips, catches with both hands and passes back for the next round; when no catch is possible (or after a pending answer) he fetches a ball from the courtside cart.
- Every bone is reset to its bind pose each frame before animation (prevents accumulation on bones without tracks); automated pose checks (pelvis/head height, torso tilt, feet above floor) run over multiple rounds.

### Ball, physics and the educational shot loop
- Deterministic ball simulation (`motion.js`) with continuous collision detection against a torus rim, backboard, padded walls and floor; restitution, friction and spin coupling; net drag; rolling.
- A correct answer always scores (the make path is verified to pass cleanly through the rim); a wrong answer never scores (miss variants—front rim, back rim, side rim, rim-and-board—are simulated and verified). Verified for 12 court positions × 12 seeds in the test suite.
- The question appears at the middle of the flight; the shot clock stops so the ball, shooter (already landed), defender and camera hold together. After a wrong answer the ball leaves the make path with a C¹-continuous blend (position and velocity continuous), then follows the simulated miss exactly.
- Pending (teacher-reviewed) answers stay neutral: the ball holds, a soft halo appears, it fades out, and no score, cheer or groan plays.
- Visible spin from the simulated angular velocity; procedural 12-strand verlet net that reacts to the ball.

### Arena and crowd
- Indoor arena (procedural): FIBA court markings and dimensions, maple strip floor with grain and restrained reflections, maroon painted keys, glass backboard with frame, shooter's square, padding, stanchion and arm, orange rim, tiered stands with ~2,000 seats and aisles, LED ribbon boards (Arabic school/platform names), end-wall scoreboard showing basketball points and attempt, roof trusses and light grid, team benches, scorer's table, ball cart.
- Crowd of up to 1,500 animated spectators from 11 Rocketbox people (adults in thobe and ghutra, thobe and taqiyah, casual wear; school-age boys) drawn with GPU instancing and baked skeletal animation in ~11 draw calls. Each spectator has its own clip, phase, delay and cross-fade. States: calm, anticipation, seated/standing celebration (stand up → cheer/clap → sit down), restrained disappointment, supportive applause, return to calm.

### Sound
- New `audio.js`: synthesised ball bounces, rim clank, backboard, net swish, shoe squeaks, hand slaps, release whoosh, landing, pea whistle, applause, room tone and convolution reverb; distance/pan/low-pass cues; per-sound voice limits; existing crowd recordings used as optional murmur/cheer/groan layers. Mute and volume control (remembered locally), suspension when the page is hidden, context closed on exit.

### Camera, UI and platform
- Sports camera behind the shooter: establishing move on entry, stable playable framing, gentle push toward the hoop during flight, short celebration emphasis, smooth return; fixed camera when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set.
- New Arabic RTL interface (Tajawal font bundled locally): school and assignment title, optional subject/teacher, separate "basketball points" and "correct answers" (plus pending count and platform points when supplied), loading progress, slow-network notice, error state with retry, question card that keeps the frozen shot visible, outcome headline, summary with the exact assignment title, keyboard shortcuts (Space to shoot, 1–4 to answer in the demo), 44 px+ touch targets, portrait and landscape layouts.
- New basketball-only controller `games/basketball/game.js`; contract unchanged: `school-basketball-{ready,config,question,result,outcome,next,advance,retry,fullscreen}`, same `token`/`index` fields, `outcome` values `goal`/`wide`/`pending`, `postMessage` target is `location.origin`. Messages are accepted only from the same origin **and** `window.parent`, with the session token, the current index and in the expected phase. Optional extra config fields `subject`/`teacher` and result field `points` are displayed when present (no host change required).
- Standalone mode exposes `ManaratGameControls` (restart / end early) to the shared `js/platform-nav.js`; embedded mode leaves navigation to the platform.
- Adaptive quality: automatic tier (pixel ratio, shadow map size, crowd count, floor texture size, antialiasing) from device class and GPU name, plus a runtime governor that lowers the pixel ratio and then thins the crowd when frames are slow.
